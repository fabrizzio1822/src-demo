## Logo

Salida final: `public/images/sdc-logo-restored.png`. Recurso web: `public/images/sdc-logo.webp` con transparencia preservada.

Preserve symmetrical gold flourishes, serif lettering and sans serif tagline. Exact text: SDC JOYERIA EXCLUSIVA; PENSADA PARA VOS. Uniform antique gold #9C7847, opaque clean edges, wide horizontal composition, reasonable transparent padding. No redesign, new symbols or black rectangle.”
